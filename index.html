About Our School

A proud educational institution of Dhaka

Dhanmondi Government Boys' High School is a government educational institution located in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The school has been serving students and the wider community for decades and has become an important part of the educational landscape of the capital city.

Established in 1965, the institution has a long history of academic education, student development and extracurricular activities. Through different generations, students have entered the school with dreams, worked hard through their school years and moved forward to higher education and professional life.

The school environment aims to encourage students to develop not only academic knowledge but also discipline, responsibility, confidence, leadership and good character. Education is viewed as a foundation for building responsible citizens who can contribute positively to society.

The school also has a strong connection with sports, cultural programs, educational activities, examinations, publications and other activities that make school life more meaningful and memorable.

Our History

Decades of education and tradition

The history of Dhanmondi Government Boys' High School is connected with the development of modern education in Dhaka. The institution was established in 1965 and has continued its educational journey through different periods of Bangladesh's history.

For generations, the school has provided students with opportunities to learn, participate, compete and develop their abilities. The school has grown alongside the surrounding area and remains a familiar name within Dhaka's educational community.

The physical infrastructure of the institution also reflects its long history. Government records identify school buildings and associated facilities dating from the establishment period.
