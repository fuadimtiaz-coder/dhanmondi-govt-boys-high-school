School History

Dhanmondi Government Boys' High School is a historic government educational institution located in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

The institution began its journey in 1965 and has served generations of students. Through the years, teachers, administrators, students and members of the wider school community have contributed to its development.

The school has continued to focus on academic education, discipline, character development, extracurricular activities, sports and preparation for higher education.
